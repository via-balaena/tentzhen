PROJECTS / THE OPEN FAB

HACKER FAB ADJACENT

To TSMC and ASML, on a Hacker Fab budget

Hacker Fab's open stack beside the industry's, row by row, and the gap between them. Our tools are to be engineered and built here, in Rust and CortenForge where they can be, but for those the safety rules keep bought.

We build on Hacker Fab's open designs and send back what we add. We are not part of Hacker Fab, and it has not reviewed this page. Safety comes first: tools on mains are bought, listed and legal, and every step runs under its own safety plan (fab.md, "Safety first"). Where a bought tool is what stops the next stage, making our own, at a fraction of the cost and meeting every safety mark, is weighed.
